## Pilot operation

1. Create one ad per creative with equal maximum budgets.
2. Start at the platform CPM floor and raise only on placements with zero delivery.
3. Stop a creative-placement pair after 1,500 views with no bot starts.
4. Stop after 10 bot starts with no signup; inspect destination-message fit first.
5. Do not scale on clicks, starts, or Telegram joins alone.
6. Scale combinations that produce proof verification, watch/alert activation,
   paid users, and confirmed revenue relative to the rest of this cohort.
7. Review at 24, 48, and 72 hours. Do not change copy and placement inside the
   same test cell; create a similar ad so attribution remains interpretable.

## Policy gate

- No guaranteed return, profit, insider-tip, urgency, or "best" claims.
- The paid plan's price and terms must remain visible before checkout.
- Ad language must match the destination and targeted channel.
- The bot must stay responsive on mobile and desktop.
- Keep every destination message consistent with the advertised proof-first flow.
