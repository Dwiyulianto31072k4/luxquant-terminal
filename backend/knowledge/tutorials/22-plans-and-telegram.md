## Free, after you log in

You get [Signals](/signals) with **finished wins (TP4)** as public proof, [Watchlist](/watchlist), [Performance](/performance), [Pulse](/market-pulse), [News](/crypto-news), [Journal](/journal), [Bitcoin](/bitcoin), [Markets](/markets), this course, notifications, and referral. That is enough to verify the process and decide whether the paid layer is for you.

## Premium

Live entry / TP / SL, [Terminal](/terminal), [AI Research](/ai-arena), [Agent](/agent), [Money Flow](/money-flow), [On-Chain](/onchain), [Order Book](/orderbook), [Calendar](/calendar), [Delistings](/delistings), [Portfolio](/portfolio), API keys. Free proof is the finished win. Live plans stay paid.

Plans are on [Pricing](/pricing): Monthly, Annual, Lifetime, paid in **USDT on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20)**. Confirmation is on-chain; activation is automatic when the payment lands. Open invoices and expiry reminders show in the bell and on the account.

## Telegram is optional

About seven in ten accounts never link Telegram. The terminal is the product. You can use all of the above in the browser.

What Telegram still is:

- A **free public channel** (`t.me/LuxQuantSignal`) — English, a funnel, not a second terminal.
- A **VIP group** for paying members who linked an account — invite / kick / grace are automatic with the subscription.
- **DMs** for alerts, only if you connected `telegram_id`.

Linking Telegram is useful (alerts you will actually see), not required. The in-app bell is the floor so Google- and Discord-only accounts are not locked out of their own product.

Manage the plan under the avatar → **Subscription**, or [Pricing](/pricing).
