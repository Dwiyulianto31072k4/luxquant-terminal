[AI Research](/ai-arena) is the **BTC Compass** workspace. Most alt calls are a Bitcoin trade in costume, so the 24-hour BTC read sits on Signals as well as here.

## What it is allowed to show

- **Bias + confidence** — Bullish / Bearish / Neutral, with a percent. Higher is more conviction, not more certainty.
- **Tactical 24h** and a **72h swing** view, plus cycle context (phase, not a trade).
- **Invalidation** — the price where the thesis is wrong. A read without an invalidation is a vibe.
- **Magnets / zones** — where liquidity sits, drawn on the projection chart against live candles.
- **What's driving it** — price action, derivatives, liquidation liquidity, each bullish or bearish.
- **Data quality** — fresh, stale, or unavailable, per source. Unavailable is not Neutral. Neutral means the evidence balanced. Unavailable means we did not have the evidence.

The Signals panel's **Alt Exposure** line (e.g. "Range only") is this same read, translated into how to treat the calls below.

## What it is forbidden to show

This is the written contract, not a vibe:

- no setup grades (A / B / C)
- no buy or sell commands
- no exact entry, stop, take-profit, or position size
- no claims of certainty or expected profit

*"It is not a signal service."* If a Compass card ever looks like a call, the card is wrong.

## How to use the workspace

Read **The Read** first. Check the **Projection Chart** for target area and invalidation. Use the **Verdict Ledger** to see how recent reads resolved. Open **Brain** only when you want the drivers. **Compass Reports** are the full write-ups, downloadable as PDF.

News may adjust confidence or raise an event warning. It must not manufacture a direction.
