## Shariah screening

Off by default, on purpose — a hidden filter that empties the board with no explanation is how people think the product is broken.

Turn it on under avatar → [Profile](/profile) → **Shariah screening**. Three modes: **Off** (default), **Show results** (Passed and Disputed both tradeable), **Strict** (Passed only). The wording never headlines Halal/Haram. Labels on a coin are screening results: Passed, Disputed, Problematic findings, Not yet assessed, Out of scope.

When it is on, [Signals](/signals) and Terminal show a notice with how many calls were hidden. Most of the catalogue has not been read by a person. Unrated stays unrated — we do not invent a green badge to fill a hole. This is *a screening result assembled from published sources — not a fatwa.*

## API keys — [API Keys](/api-keys) (Premium)

These are keys **into LuxQuant** (public data / partner access), not your exchange trading key. The trading key lives on the [Agent](/agent) connections tab. Do not paste a trading key into the API Keys page.

Revoke anything you are not using. A key with no expiry and no scope is how accounts get borrowed.
