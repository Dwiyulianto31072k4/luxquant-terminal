If you open five tabs at random you will bounce. Use three.

## 1. [Home](/home)

Market cap, 24h volume, Fear & Greed, top gainers / losers — and **Top Gainers by LuxQuant**, which is our calls, not the open market's biggest green candles. Tap a coin for the proof card. This is a briefing, not a shopping list. Time ranges on the LuxQuant gainers let you see whether yesterday's hero is still a hero.

## 2. [Signals](/signals)

The list. Date tabs (Watchlist, All Days, Today, Yesterday), search (`BTC` matches prefixes, `BTCUSDT` is exact), then the row you actually understand. The Guide button next to Filters is the in-page legend for columns. This course is the longer version of that legend.

Free accounts: the full ladder on **finished wins** (TP4). That is the proof we put in front of you. Live calls and losses are Premium. Peak percent can still show. The long-run record on [Performance](/performance) stays visible so you can audit the number before you pay.

## 3. [Pulse](/market-pulse)

The tape of what is waking up: flash moves, volume spikes, unusual prints. Filter by source and timeframe. **Top Movers** and the heatmap answer "what is loud right now".

**Called** is the paid overlay: it keeps only movers that also have a live LuxQuant plan. Pulse itself stays free. A coin can spike without a call, and a call can sit quietly without a spike. The overlay is the intersection, not a second algorithm.

Then, if you have time: [News](/crypto-news) for *why* something might be loud (Macro and Listings move price more than most headlines), and [Watchlist](/watchlist) for the one or two calls you actually intend to follow.

**Open it:** [Home](/home)
