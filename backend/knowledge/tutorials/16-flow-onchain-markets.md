A call is a plan for one pair. These pages tell you whether the *weather* around that pair makes sense. All of them are information. None of them places a trade.

## Backdrop — [Bitcoin](/bitcoin) and [Markets](/markets)

Both are free with a login.

Bitcoin: price, dominance, Fear & Greed (0–100 — mood, not a trigger), RSI, hashrate / difficulty / mempool / fees, on-chain. Rising dominance often means money preferring BTC over alts; falling can mean alts are getting a turn. Extreme fear and extreme greed are famous for marking turns and equally famous for staying extreme.

Markets: total cap and 24h volume, a heatmap of the top ~50, trending (attention, not quality), sector performance, a coin table (All / Gainers / Losers / Volume), derivatives and liquidations. Trending is "people are searching this". It is not "this is good".

## Rotation — [Money Flow](/money-flow) (Premium)

Three tabs. **Sectors** — which narratives are attracting or losing cap (24h / 7d). **Coins** — Net Buying / Net Selling / Balanced, with intensity; coins that currently have a LuxQuant call are highlighted and open the signal. **Whale** — large transfers. Stablecoin dominance rising = cash on the sidelines; falling = cash rotating into coins. That is a temperature reading, not a timer.

## Prints — [On-Chain](/onchain) (Premium)

A live alert tape: transfer, whale, smart money, mint/burn, buy/sell, exchange deposit (often intent to sell), liquidation, security. Filter by type and by size ($10K+ up to $10M+). The whale threshold is dynamic (roughly the 95th percentile of recent alerts), so it moves with the market. Deposits to exchanges are not automatically "dump incoming". They are a reason to look.

Use these *after* you have a call on the table, not instead of one. "On-chain is bullish" plus no plan is how people market-buy a wick.
