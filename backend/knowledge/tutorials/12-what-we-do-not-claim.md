Trust in a numbers product dies when the caption and the database disagree. This is the list we hold ourselves to. Use it as a filter on anything you read about LuxQuant — landing page, Telegram, an X post, a friend.

## We will not say

- **"85.6% profitable trades"** or **"85.6% of members made money."** The public win rate is *reached at least TP1*.
- **Peak as realised profit.** +313% peak next to +13% realised is the same trade. Only one of those numbers could have been banked at a TP.
- **"Exchange-verified signals."** Entry / TP / SL are parsed from the published plan and tracked against price. That is not the same as an exchange-matched fill.
- **"BTC Compass says buy."** Compass is a market read. The contract forbids setup grades, entry/SL/TP, position size, and certainty. *"It is not a signal service."*
- **"Win rate is consistent in every market."** It is not. ~71% on ≤ −3% BTC days, ~94% on > +3% days.
- **"Thousands of traders."** We publish real user counts. Follower counts are not user counts.
- **"Support 24/7"** for the chat bubble. The desk is human. There is an away reply on purpose. The machine that is always on is the algorithm, not the inbox.
- **Profit factor / SQN style brag numbers.** They are artefacts of "highest level wins" and we do not put them on the site.

## We will say

- How many calls, how many reached TP1, how many stopped out — on a public page you can open without paying.
- Peak, labelled as peak.
- That free accounts get the public record and the **fully won (TP4)** ladders as proof, and that live levels are the paid layer.
- That you can lose money following a call, using the Agent, or doing neither.
