## The bell — [Notifications](/notifications)

Inbox and Settings. Types you will actually see: called, watchlist updates, daily results, pulse, news, pumps/dumps, subscription expiry, checkout pending / expired, Agent events (position closed, execution failed, risk limit, unprotected), and the occasional broadcast.

Settings (Layer 2) let you turn categories off so the inbox does not die of noise. A bell with 7,000 unread items is not a notification system. Turn off what you will not act on.

If you linked Telegram, some of the same events can DM you. If you did not, the bell is the channel. Check it. We do not have email.

## Ask AI

The per-page assistant. It loads the guide for the page you are on and will explain a column, a filter, or a Compass term. It **refuses personal buy/sell advice and price predictions**. That is a product rule, not a shy model. Use it as a legend. Use [Signals](/signals) for the call.

## The chat bubble

Bottom-right. That is **in-app support**, typed in the browser, answered by the team through the admin panel. There is an automatic away reply when nobody is at the desk, a nudge to the team after half an hour, and a pull-back if a reply sits unread.

It is not 24/7. It is not an AI chatbot. (Ask AI is the other button.) Do not wait on it for a live fill — that is what stops and the Agent's own limits are for.

**Open it:** [Notifications](/notifications)
