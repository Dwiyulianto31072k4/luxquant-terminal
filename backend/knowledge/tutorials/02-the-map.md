You do not need every page on day one. You need a loop.

## The daily loop (free with a login)

1. **[Home](/home)** — sentiment, market stats, and the day's standout LuxQuant performers.
2. **[Signals](/signals)** — the live call list. This is the product. Free accounts see **finished wins** (TP4) with the full ladder, so the plan can be audited. Live calls, partial takes, and losses are Premium. Peak percent can still show without the levels.
3. **[Performance](/performance)** — the timestamped track record. Verify a resolved call here before you trust a headline number.
4. **[Pulse](/market-pulse)** — what is moving *right now*. The **Called** overlay (which of those movers also has a live LuxQuant plan) is Premium.
5. **[Watchlist](/watchlist)** — the calls you starred, with live prices.
6. **[Tutorials](/tips)** — this course. Progress saves if you are signed in.

[News](/crypto-news), [Journal](/journal), [Bitcoin](/bitcoin), [Markets](/markets), [Notifications](/notifications), and [Referral](/referral) are also available after login.

## The Premium cockpit

These sit behind a plan because they are the live moat:

- **[Terminal](/terminal)** — screen, time, and size the 7-day active calls against live market data.
- **[AI Research](/ai-arena)** — BTC Compass. A market read, **not** a signal service.
- **[Agent](/agent)** — execute a call on your connected exchange.
- **[Money Flow](/money-flow)**, **[On-Chain](/onchain)**, **[Order Book](/orderbook)**, **[Calendar](/calendar)**, **[Delistings](/delistings)**, **[Portfolio](/portfolio)**.

## Two names that confuse people

- **Signals** vs **Terminal**. Signals is the list of calls. Terminal never creates a call — it is a cockpit *over* the calls that already exist.
- **Ask AI** vs the chat bubble. Ask AI is the per-page assistant (it will not tell you to buy). The bubble in the corner is **human support**, with an away reply when the desk is empty. They are not the same thing.
