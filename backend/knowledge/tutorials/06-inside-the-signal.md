Click a row on [Signals](/signals). The workspace is the same every time, so you can stop hunting.

## Header

Pair, status badge, risk badge, coin tags (Layer 1, Has Utility, and so on), BTC correlation (ρ / β), called time. **Indicator guide** and **Deep Analysis** sit here.

## Tabs

- **Chart** — live TradingView of the pair. Toggle MACD, RSI, Bollinger Bands. "How to use indicator" opens a short guide. The chart is context, not a second signal.
- **Trade** — the proof: a BEFORE (entry) chart next to an AFTER (result) chart, plus entry, last, and **Highest Price After Called**. That last number is the **peak**. It is not realised gain. There is a "Copy to AI" action if you want to paste the trade into an external tool.
- **Research** — CoinGecko fundamentals: what the project is, market data, links. Useful when you do not recognise the ticker. Not a reason to size up.
- **History** — every TP / SL print, with time and price.

The journey rail stays visible so you never lose the ladder while you switch tabs.

## Deep Analysis

Opens a facts-and-tags breakdown across **ALL / M15 / H1 / H4 / CONTEXT**. Philosophy, written on the panel: **"inform, don't decide"**. There is deliberately **no** single confidence score and **no** buy/sell rating. Copy to AI as Markdown, JSON, or a ready prompt if you want a second brain — that brain is yours, not ours.

## Trade on exchanges

Links out to the pair on supported venues, and to the original Telegram message when we have it. Opening Binance from here does not place an order.

## Compare, later

The checkbox on the far left of each row is for comparing 2–5 calls side by side. Skip it until the next lesson. One call, understood, beats five calls glanced at.
