Open [Signals](/signals). Ignore the advanced filters for now.

Pick any row. Read it left to right:

1. **Pair** — e.g. `ETHUSDT`. The coin, priced in USDT.
2. **Price** — where it trades now, and the 24h change.
3. **Entry** — the price the call was planned around.
4. **Target** — TP1 through TP4. Green distance is from the *entry*, not from here.
5. **SL** — the invalidation. If price prints this, the call is a loss.
6. **Status** — `OPEN` (still running), `TP1`–`TP4` (that target printed), `WIN` / `LOSS` (resolved).
7. **Called time** — when the plan was published. The clock starts here, not when you noticed it.

Tap the row. You now have the chart, the journey rail (SL → Entry → TP1 → TP2 → TP3 → TP4), and the history of every update. That modal is the source of truth for this one call.

## Three things not to do on the first visit

- **Do not sort by the biggest green percentage and assume that is profit.** Peak run-up is not realised gain. Module 03 covers this on purpose.
- **Do not take four calls that all print 0.85 correlation to Bitcoin and call it diversification.** That is one BTC bet with extra steps. Compare exists for this.
- **Do not turn the Agent on.** Read a call, then read the numbers, then decide whether you even want automation.

If you are on a free account, open a **finished win** (status WIN / TP4). That ladder is the public proof — a receipt you can check, not a live trade. Live calls and losses are Premium. Age does not unlock them.

Star one call you actually want to follow. That is the whole first session: one row understood, one star, and a reason to come back.
