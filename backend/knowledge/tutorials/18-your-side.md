The algorithm's record and your record will not match. These three pages are how you stop pretending they do.

## [Watchlist](/watchlist)

Two modes, because they are different jobs:

- **Watching** — coins you are waiting for. A call on that pair later can notify you. Saving a coin is not an order, and it is not a promise it will be called.
- **Tracking** — starred **active calls**. Star a row on Signals or in the modal. Filter All / Open / TP Hit / Closed Win / Closed Loss. Sort by recently added, P&L, or pair.

This is the anti-firehose: two or five things you mean to follow, instead of 90 calls a day. Journal WR will not match the algorithm's WR. That gap is the point.

## [Journal](/journal)

A diary, not a broker statement. Log planned vs actual entry and exit, size, P&L (it calculates % and R:R including fees), strategy tags (including **LuxQuant Signal**), confluence, **mistake tags** (Early Exit, Moved SL, Oversized, FOMO, Revenge, No Plan, Ignored SL, Added to Loser), and emotions.

You can send a signal straight into a new entry — pair and planned ladder pre-filled. The Analytics tab is *your* win rate, equity curve, and heatmap. Be honest on the losers. The point is the leak you repeat, not a pretty curve.

## [Portfolio](/portfolio) (Premium)

Read-only view of a connected Binance account: balances, futures positions, size, entry, unrealised PnL, leverage, isolated vs cross. Empty usually means the API key is missing or invalid. It does not trade.

## Three more risk pages, when you need them

- **[Calendar](/calendar)** — macro (CPI, FOMC), token unlocks, crypto events, tagged High / Medium / Low. High-impact days are when plans get run over.
- **[Delistings](/delistings)** — exchange removal announcements, deadline, and whether we called something after the news. A delist is a liquidity event, not a discount.
- **[Order Book](/orderbook)** — bids, asks, imbalance, walls, spread. Walls get pulled. Use it to judge whether the printed ladder is even tradable, not to front-run a spoof.
