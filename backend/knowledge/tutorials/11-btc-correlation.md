Most alts are a Bitcoin trade with a different ticker. The **BTC Corr** column exists so you can see that instead of discovering it in a dump.

## Two Greek letters

- **ρ (rho)** — correlation, −1 to +1. How tightly this pair moves *with* BTC. Near +1 = same direction, same days. Near 0 = not tracking. Negative is rare and usually short-lived.
- **β (beta)** — amplitude. How *hard* it moves when BTC moves. β > 1 means it amplifies Bitcoin. A 2% BTC day can be a 6% day on a high-β small cap — both ways.

**Decoupled** (the ⚡ filter) is a statistical call: z-score above 2 and correlation below 0.5. Often a coin-specific catalyst — listing, unlock, narrative. It is not a safety rating. A decoupled coin can still go to zero without Bitcoin's help.

**High align** (🔥, alignment ≥ 70) means this pair is behaving like a BTC proxy. Taking four of those is not four ideas.

## How to use it on a real morning

1. Read [AI Research](/ai-arena) for the 24h BTC bias — range, trend, or "don't pretend this is a trend day".
2. On [Signals](/signals), decide whether you want **high-align** (you are expressing the BTC view) or **decoupled** (you are expressing something else).
3. Before you size two "different" alts, Compare them. If both ρ ≥ 0.70, it is one bet.

Win rate itself moves with Bitcoin. Violent down days have printed ~71% hit rate; violent up days ~94%. The column is there so you stop being surprised by that.
