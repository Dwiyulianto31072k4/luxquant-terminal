LuxQuant Terminal is a quantitative crypto market-intelligence platform. The product **does** generate algorithmic trade **calls** — a pair, an entry, take-profits (TP1–TP4), a stop, a timestamp, and a public outcome. Those live on [Signals](/signals).

Around each call there is context: how the coin has behaved before, how it moves with Bitcoin, where capital is rotating, what on-chain wallets are doing, and a 24-hour Bitcoin read from BTC Compass. The point of the terminal is that a decision starts from that record, not from a screenshot in a group chat.

## What it is not

- **Not a promise that you will make money.** A call that reached TP1 is a win on our books. Your fill, your size, your fees, and whether you actually took the trade are yours.
- **Not a buy/sell command.** Verdicts, scores, and Compass bias are descriptive. They do not tell you to enter.
- **Not a broker.** The terminal does not hold your funds. The **Agent** can place orders on *your* exchange account (Binance, BingX, Bitget, Bybit, OKX, or Gate) if you connect a key and turn it on. Until then, nothing executes.
- **Not Telegram-only.** Most accounts sign in with Google. Signals, the bell, chat, and the track record all work in the browser. Telegram is optional — a free public channel, a VIP group for subscribers who linked an account, and DMs if you connected one.

## The one sentence to keep

A **call** is a timestamped trade plan with a public outcome. Everything else on LuxQuant — Terminal, Compass, flow, on-chain, the Agent — is there to help you *read* that plan, not to replace your judgement.

This course exists because the gap between what a number looks like and what it actually measures is the easiest way to misuse the product. Module 03 is the one that closes that gap.
